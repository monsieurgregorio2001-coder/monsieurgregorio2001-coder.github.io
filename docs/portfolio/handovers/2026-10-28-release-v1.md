# 2026-10-28: Release V1

Release V1 is the Show Your Work launch (DIRECTION section 10, phase 5). This note says what shipped, where it lives, and what the owner still has to do.

## What shipped

- House 2.0.0 and 2.0.1, immutable folders under `/house/`. Every site runs on 2.0.1.
- The hub foyer with the launch headline "I build AI tools you can check." and the subhead "Four projects. Each one lets you check its claim." The old line stays in `site.json` as `fallback`. Contact heading is "Work with me". About is unchanged.
