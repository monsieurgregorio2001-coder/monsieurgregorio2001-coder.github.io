# Job search and qualification

Use the private candidate profile and [tracker contract](tracker-contract.md). This is an execution procedure for the next agent; the planning checkpoint did not populate a shortlist.

## Inputs

Broad role-family direction is confirmed in [positioning](../roadmap/positioning.md). Remaining inputs include seniority, current location and relevant work authorization, countries and arrangements, employee/contractor preference, schedule overlap, compensation floor and currency/basis, start availability, employer exclusions, and the evidence available for required skills. Keep unknowns explicit. Research can proceed before every preference is settled; hard questions must be settled before they are used in an application answer.

## Source strategy

Start with employer career pages and their linked ATS postings. Use LinkedIn, job boards, search engines, and network leads for discovery; verify the requisition and apply destination against an employer-controlled source. An aggregator's label does not establish hiring eligibility or current availability. Use official source URLs and preserve the requisition ID.

Search all confirmed families: AI engineering; AI product building/rapid prototyping (including vibe-coding opportunities); AI solutions architecture/engineering; and GTM/growth/revenue-systems engineering. Include adjacent titles when the work fits the underlying problem-solving/product/systems direction. Keep one common candidate story and choose a specific application angle.

Suggested aliases: `AI Engineer`, `Applied AI Engineer`, `LLM Engineer`, `AI Product Engineer`, `AI Automation Engineer`, `AI Product Builder`, `AI Prototyping Engineer`, `Vibe Coder`, `AI Solutions Architect`, `AI Solutions Engineer`, `GTM Engineer`, `Growth Engineer`, and `Revenue Systems Engineer`. These are discovery terms; judge the live responsibilities and required evidence rather than treating all aliases as equivalent. Pair location filters with actual eligibility checks. Seniority remains unconfirmed and must match the source evidence; research need not wait for a rigid title list.

For architecture/solutions roles, inspect actual discovery, stakeholder, integration, and delivery requirements. For GTM roles, inspect the commercial workflow, CRM/data/integration requirements and expected business outcomes. Broad user interest is not proof of experience in every lane. The [positioning matrix](../roadmap/positioning.md) distinguishes evidence to reuse and gaps to resolve.

Keep a small preferred-employer watchlist based on confirmed constraints, useful products, relevant engineering work, and hiring availability. Do not build an elaborate database before qualifying the first five opportunities. No recurring monitor was scheduled by this planning session.

## Capture a real opportunity

1. Open the official description, inspect the application endpoint, and record checked_at, employer, exact title, requisition ID, location/arrangement, and close date if stated.
2. Deduplicate against the tracker using employer plus stable requisition ID. Different locations or mirrors may share a requisition; different requisitions at one employer are distinct but should not trigger indiscriminate duplicate applications.
3. Save a private source snapshot with requirements and the original URL. Capture the posted date only if the employer states it; discovery time is not the posting date.
4. Record must-have requirements separately from preferences, and map relevant proof before spending time tailoring.
5. Recheck the live official source before finalizing and submitting. A cached search result is insufficient.

## Hard checks before scoring

| Check | Record pass, fail, or unknown, with evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Hiring location | Candidate's actual location is within the employer's permitted countries/states; remote does not mean worldwide |
| Work authorization / sponsorship | Candidate facts and the posting's requirements are compatible; do not infer status from citizenship, a travel folder, or a remote label |
| Work arrangement and schedule | Onsite/hybrid obligations, required overlap, travel, and contract type fit confirmed preferences |
| Essential scope | Non-negotiable seniority, regulated requirements, language, credential, or experience constraints can be answered truthfully |
| Compensation | Published range is compatible, or it is unknown and marked for later clarification; do not fabricate a range |
| Employer legitimacy and status | Company/source/application channel are consistent and the role is accepting applications |
| Candidate preferences | Exclusions, start-date constraints, and other confirmed deal-breakers are respected |

A hard fail excludes or archives with a reason. Unknown location/work-authorization/essential qualification is a qualification blocker, not a scoring discount. Undisclosed salary can remain unknown while research continues; only Garfield can set or change a compensation preference. Do not guess application form answers to clear a gate.

## Proposed fit rubric

After hard checks, score each dimension 0–5 with a short rationale and supporting claim IDs. Scores are prioritization aids, not a prediction of being hired.

| Dimension | Weight | What strong evidence looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work/technical match | 30 | Responsibilities and technical requirements match the lane and demonstrated capability |
| Demonstrated relevant delivery | 25 | Credible projects/employment evidence covers key responsibilities, with ownership and limits |
| Scope and seniority | 20 | Expected autonomy and leadership scope match evidenced responsibility |
| Practical preferences | 15 | Confirmed work arrangement, schedule, and other practical preferences support the opportunity |
| Career direction | 10 | The work serves the candidate's confirmed interests and next step |

Total = sum(weight × score / 5), out of 100. Starting convention: 75+ prioritize; 60–74 deliberate stretch with a named gap and rationale; below 60 defer. If any dimension cannot be assessed, keep fit.score null and use a reasoned provisional priority; do not invent a number or enthusiasm. Record confidence and assessed evidence when comparing records. Do not override a hard fail with a high total. Tune the rubric from real outcomes.

## Ready to tailor

The record has a current official source, stable ID, no unresolved blocking hard check, a documented fit rationale, a gap strategy, a chosen resume lane, and a next action. Keep at most two packets in active tailoring. If a posting is unusually time-sensitive, triage it immediately without abandoning factual checks.

## Sourcing output

The initial goal is five qualified opportunities rather than a large bookmark pile. Each should have an explicit apply/defer/discard reason and relevant proof. Record a small number of useful disqualifications too, so repeated bad sources and misunderstood location labels can be corrected.
