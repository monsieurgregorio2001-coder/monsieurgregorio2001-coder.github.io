# ADR 0004 — PaperLab is the flagship project

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-10-15

## Context

The 2026-10-15 audit found that four equal-weight cards leave nothing as the lead. The owner was asked to pick one. Candidates: Living Mindmap (closest to agent/MCP roles), Slop Editorial (most visual), Paluwagan (strongest test story), PaperLab (theory and teaching, cleanest demo).

## Decision

PaperLab leads the Work section with a featured treatment: full-width block, larger framed screenshot, the proof numbers set large, and a direct "Open the demo" call to action. The remaining three keep the standard card. Order after PaperLab: Living Mindmap, Slop Editorial, Online Paluwagan.

Owner's reasoning, recorded from the session: PaperLab is the clearest demonstration that he understands the model internals he builds on, which underwrites the "Applied AI" claim better than any single product. It is also the one that works instantly in a browser with no setup.

## Consequences

- Hero proof points should include one PaperLab figure (Lighthouse 96/100/100/100 or the 19 math tests) alongside the 12 MCP tools line.
- The PaperLab case page is written first and sets the template for the other three.
- The featured block needs a dedicated hero crop of the attention heatmap, shot at 2× for sharpness.
