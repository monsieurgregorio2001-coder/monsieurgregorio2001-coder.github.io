# ADR 0016: No sound and no lab page

Status: proposed by Claude on the owner's delegation, 2026-10-20. Reversible.
Date: 2026-10-20

## Context

Sound and a `/lab/` page were proposed. All three judges found they prove nothing.

## Decision

Neither ships. Sound in any form and a `/lab/` page are out of scope. The only lab paths are `/lab/doorway/a/` and `/b/`, unlinked `noindex` fixtures for the doorway bench, outside the sitemap.

## Consequences

- Fewer moving parts on the hub.
- See DIRECTION.md 5.7 and 10.
