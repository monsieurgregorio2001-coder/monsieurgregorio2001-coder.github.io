# ADR 0014: Hero claims

Status: proposed by Claude on the owner's delegation, 2026-10-20. Reversible.
Date: 2026-10-20

## Context

Unverified career claims must stay off new surfaces. The hero carried a "Now" line and an "8 developers" claim.

## Decision

The "Now" line and "8 developers" leave the first screen. About stays unchanged (ADR 0008). Proof figures do not appear in the hero.

The hero part already shipped in the phase-0 honesty sweep on branch `phase-0/honesty-sweep`.

## Consequences

- The first screen states only checkable facts.
- See DIRECTION.md 9 and 10.
