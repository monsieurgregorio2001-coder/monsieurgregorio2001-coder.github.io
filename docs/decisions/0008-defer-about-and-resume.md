# ADR 0008 — Defer the About photo, career arc and resume link

Status: accepted (deferral)
Date: 2026-10-15

## Context

The audit recommended a photo, a short career arc and a resume PDF link in About. The owner chose to handle these later, after the resume is finalised in the separate `Professionalism` repo.

## Decision

Leave the About section's content unchanged in this initiative. Styling changes that apply to the whole page (spacing, hover craft, the "now" line) still apply to About. Track the deferred items in `TRACKER.md` under "Later".

## Consequences

- The hero and footer do not link a PDF yet.
- When the resume ships, add it at `/resume.pdf`, link it from the hero actions and footer, and add it to the sitemap.

Amended 2026-10-28 by the owner: About keeps its roles but drops the career numbers ("eight", "four", "six months"). The photo, career arc and resume stay deferred.
