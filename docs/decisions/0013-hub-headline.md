# ADR 0013: Hub headline

Status: proposed by Claude on the owner's delegation, 2026-10-20. Reversible.
Date: 2026-10-20

## Context

The owner's headline "I turn messy operations into working AI systems." fits only Paluwagan. The four projects share a habit instead: each lets a visitor check its claim.

## Decision

The new H1 "I build AI tools you can check." ships at launch, once all four toys are live and all slots are green. Until then the owner's line stays. It is also the fallback in `site.json`. The lede is "Four projects. Each one lets you check its claim."

## Consequences

- The new line is true only when every room is checkable.
- Headline and Open Graph copy change together.
- See DIRECTION.md 1, 5.2, and 7.
