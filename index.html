    Online Paluwagan

    A demo rotating-savings group with a reconciling ledger, a seeded payout draw, and pre-written reminder templates a person approves before anything is sent.

    Problem
    Paluwagan groups run on chat threads and memory, so late payments and disputes are common.
    Build
    An append-only ledger in integer centavos, a seeded payout draw, rule-based risk flags, and pre-written reminder templates in English and Filipino that a person approves.
    Result
    53 tests, including 12,000 randomized ledger operations, arrears included, that always reconcile.
    • React
    • TypeScript
    • Ledger
